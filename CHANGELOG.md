# LFC Foundry organization page changelog

What changed on the organization page (github.com/lfcfoundry), newest first, in plain words.

## 2026-11-22
- The LFC-pack entry now names 0.24.0 (its numbers are still the ones measured at 0.12.3).

## 2026-11-21
- The new guess-gate module is on the page, which now counts 55 modules and tools, and the LFC-pack entry names 0.15.0 (its numbers are still the ones measured at 0.12.3).

## 2026-11-20
- The new netflow-chord module (a 1.6 KB chord layout) is on the page, which now counts 54 modules and tools.
- The LFC-pack entry now names version 0.14.7 and the newer Parquet numbers (files 0.5 to 0.7 the size of Parquet at zstd level 3; row reads about 2 to 6 times faster than the Parquet readers), and the lz4-kit entry names 1.1.2.
- The lz4-kit entry now has the 1.1.1 numbers: faster than every other LZ4 library tested at compressing and decompressing, not just a tie.
- The wake-keeper module is removed from the page, which now counts 53 modules and tools.
- The app file sizes are re-measured on the main branch: Pineapple Defense 2.75 MB, Apple Press 0.75 MB and Story Editor 0.32 MB (they had grown since the last check).

## 2026-11-19
- The new lz4-kit module is on the page (in the main branch, not yet released), with its speed against the browser's gzip and other LZ4 libraries, and its larger output. The page now counts 54 modules and tools.

## 2026-11-14 (evening)
- The "What is new" section now names LFC-pack 0.10.35, and the Netflow Map entry adds the faster nested views (first picture and view switching on a 50,000-item map, with Chromium 156 and Firefox 158 numbers).

## 2026-11-14 (later)
- The "What is new" section now names LFC-pack 0.10.32 instead of 0.10.25.

## 2026-11-14
- The LFCP Workbench entry now has real numbers: a 300 MB, 7-million-row file imports faster and is saved 58% smaller, sorts by a number column 3 to 5 times faster than before, and a 100 MB file passed on an Android phone. It says plainly that DuckDB-WASM is still faster at sorting.
- A new "What is new" section covers LFC-pack 0.10.25, the new zstd-kit module, Netflow Map's Sift choice and Credits button, the Thumb Circuit story campaign, the LFCP Workbench, and the new credits pages.
- Thumb Circuit and Netflow Map join the app list and the play links; the page now counts 6 apps and games and 53 modules and tools.
- The module list is the current one, with zstd-kit added.

## 2026-10-30
- The page now opens with a banner and four app cards drawn as pictures, in light and dark. They read well on a phone.
- The app and module lists are lists instead of wide tables, so nothing is cut off on a phone.
- Play links go to each game's play page; Pineapple Defense and Story Editor are listed with their sizes.

## 2026-10-29
- The page says plainly that the apps need no install, no administrator rights and no special computer knowledge; the code modules are for developers.
- A new top section and the module list (49 modules and tools) in the same look as the app pages.
