# LFC Foundry organization page changelog

What changed on the organization page (github.com/lfcfoundry), newest first, in plain words.

## 2026-10-30
- The page now opens with a banner and four app cards drawn as pictures, in light and dark. They read well on a phone.
- The app and module lists are lists instead of wide tables, so nothing is cut off on a phone.
- Play links go to each game's play page; Pineapple Defense and Story Editor are listed with their sizes.

## 2026-10-29
- The page says plainly that the apps need no install, no administrator rights and no special computer knowledge; the code modules are for developers.
- A new top section and the module list (49 modules and tools) in the same look as the app pages.
